An English page

Everything you are reading uses a small fraction of the characters inside this font. The page only needs the letters, numbers and punctuation of ordinary English, plus a few accented characters like café, résumé and naïve.

What the browser downloads

  • One font request, for the complete file.
  • Latin, Latin Extended, Greek and Cyrillic glyphs, even though none of this page's text needs the last three.
  • Hinting and layout data for every one of those glyphs.

Try it

Open the browser's network panel, filter to Font, turn on Disable cache, and reload. Note the size of the single font request, then open the After page and compare.
