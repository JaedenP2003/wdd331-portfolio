Typography Tokens

A major-third modular scale, fluid from 320px to 1200px, mapped to semantic roles. Resize the window and watch the numbers change.

Viewport: px

The scale

Each step is the one before times 1.25. The small steps stay fixed. The large steps use clamp() with a linear preferred value, so each one is exactly its minimum at 320px and its maximum at 1200px.

Roles

Components never use a step directly. They use a role, and each role points at one step.

--font-size-body = md, system font

Body text uses the system font stack at 16px with an open line height, so long passages stay easy to read at every width.

Fonts

  • Display: The Wild Breath of Zelda, self-hosted Latin subset (10 KB).
  • Headings: Triforce, self-hosted Latin subset (19 KB).
  • Body and captions: the system font stack.
  • Loading: font-display: swap. The fallback serif shows right away and swaps when the font arrives.
